### 📱 Featured Project: Habit Tracker - Us+

Available on both **Google Play** and the **App Store**. Us+ was built on a simple truth: most habit trackers fail because building routines in isolation is lonely. We created a platform centered around social accountability to make staying consistent easier and more engaging.

**Key Features & Technical Implementation:**
- 🤝 **Shared Habits & Real-time Sync:** Track shared habits with partners with low-latency Supabase Realtime synchronization.
- 📱 **Interactive Home Screen Widgets:** Native widgets built for Android (Kotlin) and iOS (Swift & WidgetKit) to log habits without opening the app.
- 🎨 **Social Engagement:** Dynamic GIF reactions powered by KLIPY integration.
- 🔔 **Push Notifications & Auth:** Multi-platform push notifications (FCM & APNs) and secure authentication, including Sign in with Apple.
- 💎 **In-App Subscriptions:** Cross-platform monetization and subscription state management via RevenueCat.
